Base-game flow

A round may contain multiple paid spins while walking Collectors remain active.

1Choose a configured reelset and one random stop per reel.
2Overlay walking Collectors carried from the previous paid spin.
3Evaluate paylines, assign visible coin credits, and pay Collectors.
4Draw 0-4 jackpot overlays on eligible symbols and increment their progressives.
5If Bag triggers are visible, the full game launches the feature immediately.
6Move active Collectors right and continue paid spins until all have exited.

Full-game feature flow

Trigger Bags are transferred directly from the triggering base window without deduplication.

1Pass every visible trigger Bag from the base result, preserving duplicates and board order.
2Place the starting Bags and credit coins in the initially unlocked rows.
3Land natural coins or Bags, then resolve Bags in configured order.
4Attach jackpot-token overlays only to eligible naturally landed feature coins.
5Collect session-local jackpot meters; reaching a target awards that jackpot type.
6The full game pays the current progressive and resets only the awarded jackpot to seed.
